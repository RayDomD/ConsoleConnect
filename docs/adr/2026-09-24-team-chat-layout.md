# Team chat layout

Status: accepted visual direction

Team chat uses a Messenger-style conversation within Console Connect's Paper & ink Review Desk. Messages show the sender and time, the current member's messages align to the right, and the composer stays at the bottom. The workspace and team rails remain visible on desktop. The selected design is [the team chat mockup](../mockups/2026-09-24-team-chat-messenger.html).

Replies, editing, unsending, unread state, and notifications are planned separately. Ordinary chat remains distinct from official decisions and task review.
