# Office: seeing each other at work

Status: accepted

The Office is a workspace-level space where the team sees who is here, what each person is working on, and a glimpse of their console. It builds on the existing view-only terminal sharing and keeps the specification's rules: sharing is optional and controlled by the session owner, watchers cannot type, and comments go to task discussion.

## Decision (Q17)

Rooms that arrange themselves. Each person appears as a card in the room that matches their state, and the card moves when that state changes:

- **Building:** a task console is running.
- **Needs a hand:** their tool is waiting for input, stuck, or out of usage.
- **Reviewing:** work is waiting for their review.
- **Planning:** an orchestrator session is running.
- **Around:** present with nothing running, shown compactly with presence (here, away).

Cards show the person, task, tool, and a three-line console glimpse when the person shares it. A private console shows only name, task, and room. Clicking a shared card opens the full view-only viewer with the task discussion beside it. "Ask to watch" requests access to a private console. When someone watches another's console, a huddle marker shows both avatars on that card. Cards ease into a new room over about 400ms, with no motion under reduced-motion settings.

The selected design is [the Office mockup](../mockups/2026-09-25-office.html). The alternatives considered (a wall of terminals and a hand-arranged floor plan) are in [the options mockup](../mockups/2026-09-25-office-options.html).

## Sharing default and control (Q18)

- **Ask once, then remember.** The first console launch after the Office arrives asks "Show this console in the Office?" with Show in Office, Keep private, and a checked "Remember my choice". The answer becomes the person's default for new consoles, changeable in Settings › Office. See [the default options mockup](../mockups/2026-09-25-office-share-default.html).
- **One accessible switch.** The console strip carries a `role="switch"` control labelled "Shown in Office" or "Private", so the state is never shown by color alone. Space and Enter toggle it. It has a visible focus ring and a hit area of at least 32px. `Ctrl+Shift+S` toggles it when the terminal is not focused. The Office header's "Show my console here" is the same control for the current session. See [the toggle mockup](../mockups/2026-09-25-office-share-toggle.html).
- **Turning it off.** Viewers' streams end immediately, and their viewer says "Sam stopped sharing this console" through a polite live region, with Open task and Ask to watch. The person's card keeps presence, task, and room but shows "console is private". The owner gets a status message with an Undo reachable by keyboard.
- **Pause sharing.** Output can be hidden briefly, for example while a tool prints keys, then resumed.

## Constraints

- Glimpses update a few times a minute. The full live stream runs only while a viewer is open, following the spec's "limit and batch optional live streams".
- Presence (here, away, offline) is new.
- View-only sharing on Supabase-hosted workspaces is still unbuilt and is required for the Office there.
