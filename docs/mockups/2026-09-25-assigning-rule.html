Q11 · Roles, revisited

Hierarchy is about who can assign work to whom. The orchestrator is just a faster pen.

You're right to protect the hierarchy. The key fact is that today any member can already create tasks and assign them to anyone by hand in the app (I checked the code: only approving decisions and accepting packages are role-restricted). Locking only the orchestrator would block the tool but not the action. If hierarchy matters, the control has to sit on assigning, and then it applies to the app and the orchestrator equally.

What protects the team today

Assigned work is only a request until the recipient approves it, and a person with the right role has to accept what comes back.

Anyone assigns→Recipient approves→Work→Owner or Reviewer accepts

What's missing for a hierarchy

Nothing stops a Contributor from sending work to the Owner, or to another Contributor, even though the recipient can decline. That's the gap to close, and it isn't specific to AI.

Casey (Contributor)→ assigns →Blair (Owner)?
