Q9 · The auto-handle setting

How much control should the auto setting give?

Q8 settled it: events are typed in but not sent by default, and auto-handling is an opt-in setting. This screen asks how fine-grained that setting is. Both options live in Settings › Orchestrator, are off by default, and are stored per person on their own computer. The switches work.
