Built to be accessible

  • A real switch: role="switch" with aria-checked. Space and Enter toggle it, and it's reachable with Tab
  • The label always says the state in words, "Shown in Office" or "Private", so it never relies on color alone
  • A visible focus ring, and a 32px hit area around the 30px track
  • A status message announces the change ("Console hidden from the Office"), with Undo reachable by keyboard
  • The watcher's viewer announces "Sam stopped sharing" through a polite live region
  • The thumb movement is 180ms, with none under reduced-motion settings
