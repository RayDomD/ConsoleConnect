How rooms are chosen

Building: a console is running. Needs a hand: a tool is waiting for input, stuck, or out of usage. Reviewing: has work waiting for their review. Planning: an orchestrator session. Around: here, with nothing running.

Huddles

When someone watches another's console, their avatars sit together on that card. It's a quiet signal that people are working together.

Motion with purpose

A card eases into its new room over about 400ms when state changes, so you notice the move. There's no motion under reduced-motion settings.

Same rules as before

Sharing is per person and view-only. Private consoles show name, task, and room only. Glimpses are light, and full streams run only while open.
